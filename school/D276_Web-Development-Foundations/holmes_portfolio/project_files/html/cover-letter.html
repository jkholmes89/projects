Cover Letter

To the Hiring Team,

I am writing to introduce myself as an emerging software engineer with a systems-first mindset. Currently pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Western Governors University, I am seeking opportunities to apply my blend of operational discipline and technical creativity to build secure, scalable software.

My transition into software development is built on a strong foundation of practical experience. Holding an Associate’s degree in Computer Systems Technology and having worked in IT support, I understand the infrastructure that code relies on. Years of troubleshooting live environments have instilled in me a "reliability-first" approach to engineering. I do not just view software as lines of code, but as a component of a larger system that must be robust, secure, and maintainable in production.

This background directly influences my current academic focus. While I am mastering backend development and algorithm design, I am simultaneously researching the intersection of DevSecOps and AI governance. I believe that as technology advances, the responsibility to implement it safely increases. My long-term professional goal is to contribute to the ethical and secure deployment of AI, and I am eager to start that journey by writing high-quality, defensible code today.

I offer the technical curiosity of a student combined with the professional maturity of an experienced IT specialist. I am looking to join a team that values continuous learning and pragmatic problem-solving, where I can contribute to the development lifecycle while continuing to grow as an engineer.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how my background and skills can add value to your organization.

Sincerely,
